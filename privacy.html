Effective date: November 8, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you visit JohnNelsonPlumbing.com or call the telephone number shown here. This is an independently operated plumbing information and lead-generation website. The website operator does not necessarily perform plumbing services directly.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the website and how calls are handled, information may include:

  • Information you choose to provide, such as your name, contact information, location, and details of a plumbing concern.
  • Phone and caller information, such as your telephone number, caller ID, call time, and call duration, where available.
  • Information shared during a telephone inquiry, such as the service you need and any details you provide to an operator or service provider.
  • Basic technical information, such as browser type, device details, and website request logs that hosting infrastructure may process.

The current website page does not have a contact form. Other collection methods are not assumed by this policy.

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, help connect callers with relevant plumbing service providers, facilitate requested consultations or services, improve website performance and the quality of inquiries, prevent misuse, and meet applicable legal obligations.

This website is operated for lead-generation purposes. When you call the number displayed on the website, your inquiry may be routed or referred to an independent third-party plumbing business or other relevant service provider.

The website operator may receive compensation for generating or referring qualifying inquiries. Providers are independent of this website and are responsible for their own services, estimates, terms, and privacy practices.

Calling about a service does not, by itself, authorize unrelated marketing calls or messages.

5. Call Tracking and Recording

Telephone inquiries may be routed or tracked through third-party telecommunications systems. Depending on the service used, these systems may process call-related information, such as caller ID, routing details, and call duration.

Recording status must be confirmed by the website operator. This policy does not state that calls are currently recorded. If recording is enabled, appropriate notice and consent must be provided where required by applicable law.

6. Cookies and Website Analytics

The supplied website pages do not include an identifiable analytics service, advertising pixel, or cookie-setting script. Hosting providers may process basic technical request data. If analytics, cookies, or other tracking technologies are added later, this policy should be updated to describe them accurately.

7. Information Sharing

Information relating to your inquiry may be shared with a relevant independent service provider so the provider can respond. Limited information may also be processed by hosting or telecommunications vendors that help operate the website and its telephone connections. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law.

8. Data Security and Retention

Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect information handled through the website and its service providers. No internet or telephone system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Information should be kept only as long as reasonably needed for its legitimate purposes and applicable legal requirements. Actual retention arrangements depend on the systems and providers used.

9. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. To ask about information connected with your call, you may contact the current website operator using a contact method the operator has verified and published.
